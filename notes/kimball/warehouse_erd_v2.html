EOY date pattern: All year-level and period-level tables join to dim_date via an end-of-year surrogate date column (vintage_date, acs_period_end_date, boundary_version_eoy). Joining on a raw year integer would fan-out — one fact row matching 365 date rows. Dashed connectors = EOY date join.
Semi-additive measures: pct_district in fct_district_year_zoning_composition must not be SUMmed across vintage years or across districts. Aggregate only within a single year slice.
fct_tract_acs as lookup: bridge_tract_district_overlap references fct_tract_acs on geoid_tract — a fact table acting as a dimensional lookup. ACS data contains measurements (estimates + MOE), so no separate dim_census_tract is needed.
Geometry separation: Polygon geometry lives in geo_district_boundaries (E8) to keep analytics joins on dim_district lightweight. Only loaded for spatial operations and map rendering.
Connector scope: Lines drawn for adjacent-column relationships only (dims → facts, fct_tract_acs → bridge). Cross-column relationships (R6, R8, R10, R11, R12) are documented in the table above — their FK fields are also labeled inline in each table box.
