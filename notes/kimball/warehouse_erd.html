fct_permits  — transaction fact table
PK permit_id varchar
FK district_id → dim_district
FK date_key → dim_date (month grain)
   permit_type varchar
   permit_status varchar
   match_method varchar · audit
FCT permit_count integer · additive
FCT permit_fee numeric · additive
fct_district_year_zoning_composition  — periodic snapshot
FK district_id → dim_district
FK date_key → dim_date (vintage_year grain)
FK zoning_code → dim_zoning_code
FCT area_sqft numeric · additive within year
FCT pct_district_area numeric · semi-additive ⚠
Conformed dimensions: dim_district and dim_date are single shared tables — not duplicated. Both fact tables hold foreign keys into the same dimension rows, enabling cross-fact slicing by district or time period without a join between the two fact tables.

dim_date join grain differs: fct_permits joins on a month-level key; fct_district_year_zoning_composition joins on a vintage-year-level key. Same physical table, different granularity of lookup.
PK Primary key FK Foreign key → dimension FCT Measure / fact column ⚠ Semi-additive: do not SUM across vintage years or across districts
