Area-Weighted Interpolation & MAUP

Task 9.5 · ACS Census Tracts → Philadelphia Planning Districts

The Problem: Two Boundary Systems That Don't Align

What area-weighting does (and why it's wrong here)


Assumption Population is uniformly distributed inside the tract. Tract is 60% in District A, 40% in District B.
Area-weighted result → 600 people to District A
→ 400 people to District B
(from 1,000 total in tract)
Reality All 1,000 residents live on the District A side (residential blocks). District B side is industrial.
Error introduced District A undercounted by 400 people.
District B overcounted by 400 people.
MAUP · L10

The Formula

Area-weighted allocation — per (tract, district) pair

-- Step 1: for each (tract, district) intersection pair allocated_value = tract_value * ( ST_Area( ST_Intersection(tract.geom, district.geom) ) / ST_Area(tract.geom) ) -- Step 2: sum contributions across all tracts per district district_value = SUM(allocated_value) OVER all tracts intersecting that district -- Source: Spatial SQL (Forrest), Section 5.1, p. 318

The area fraction (intersection area / tract area) is the weight applied to each variable in the tract. Summing across all tracts gives the district-level total. The same weight applies to every demographic variable — population, median income, renter households, etc.

Steps to Implement in PostGIS

  • 1
    Validate geometries. Run ST_IsValid() on both layers before intersecting. Invalid geometries produce incorrect or empty intersection results.
  • 2
    Intersect layers. ST_Intersection(tract.geom, district.geom) for all pairs where ST_Intersects() is true. This produces one row per (tract, district) overlap.
  • 3
    Filter slivers. Discard intersection fragments below a minimum area threshold (e.g., 1 sq meter). Slivers are tiny artifacts from boundary misalignment — near-zero area, negligible values, but they inflate row counts.
  • 4
    Compute weights. ST_Area(intersection) / ST_Area(tract) per row. Weights for a given tract should sum to ≈ 1.0 (within floating point tolerance).
  • 5
    Allocate values. Multiply each demographic variable by its weight. Record match_method = 'area_weighted' for audit trail.
  • 6
    Aggregate to district. SUM(allocated_value) GROUP BY district_id. Run sanity checks before using results in any metric.

Why No Pre-Built Crosswalk Exists

Census crosswalks exist

Tract → County
Tract → Place (city/borough)
Tract → ZCTA (ZIP code tabulation area)
Tract → PUMA

All Census-defined geographies.

Planning districts no crosswalk

Defined by the City of Philadelphia, not the Census Bureau. The Census Bureau has no record of their boundaries. No relationship file is published.

Must compute from geometry.

Tradeoff vs. crosswalk

A Census crosswalk is authoritative + pre-validated. Computing your own overlay is flexible but introduces sliver risk, depends on geometry quality, and requires the MAUP caveat. Document it as a limitation (L10).
